What to do if money was stolen

  • Contact your bank or wallet provider immediately.
  • Freeze the account or card if the transaction is unauthorized.
  • Save transaction screenshots, SMS alerts, and payment details.

What to do after phishing

  • Do not click more links from the suspicious message.
  • Reset passwords using official app links only.
  • Report the message and block the sender.

When an account is compromised

  • Change all account passwords immediately.
  • Check recovery email and phone settings.
  • Review recent activity for suspicious logins.
